Product Return & Exchange Policy

7-Days Exchange Framework

Hum apne premium clients ko 7-Days Return aur Exchange ki sahoolat dete hain. Agar aapko fabric product mein koi genuine damage ya mismatch mile, toh aap product delivery ke 7 din ke andar exchange request generate kar sakte hain.

Unstitched Fabric Condition

Wapasi ya exchange ke liye zaroori hai ke unstitched kurta fabric, luxury lawn, ya latha suit kata hua (uncut) na ho aur original packaging mein ho. Dhule hue (washed) ya kate hue fabrics exchange nahi kiye jayenge.

Verification Routes

Jaise hi aapki return entry hamare team matrix tak pahunchegi, hum parcel ko inspect karke naya article active dispatch route par rawana kar denge. Is silsile mein claims hamare live customercare helpdesk par register hote hain.
